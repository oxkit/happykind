What is the $KIND token?

Blockchain technology and cryptocurrencies have become modern solutions to several of the finance and technology sectors’ problems, significantly increasing efficiency, reducing costs, and ensuring decentralized and secure transactions. Leveraging this opportunity, we introduced the $KIND token for Happy Kind. This innovation mirrors KidZania’s kidZos, aiming to include everyone, from kids to adults, in a fun, rewarding system of earning and using tokens for personal joy and fulfillment.

$KIND is an SPL token based on the Solana blockchain, chosen for its high security & availability and low gas fees.

How do you earn $KIND token?

Earning $KIND token is a seamless process with our affiliated Happy Kind businesses — KITA Singapore, KITA Sleep Studio, Happy Kind Kiddo Singapore, and Happy Kind Kiddo Malaysia:

  1. Make your purchases from any of our affiliated businesses.
  2. On the checkout page, look for the field to enter your Solana wallet address.
  3. Complete your order.
  4. After your order is fulfilled and no refund is requested, we will promptly deposit $KIND tokens to your Solana wallet.
What can you do with $KIND token?

$KIND token holders can swap (buy/sell) their tokens on Raydium DEX for immediate gain.

Looking ahead, we hoped to enable holders to redeem $KIND as shopping credits or exchange them for physical goods within Happy Kind affiliated businesses. As we are not developers, we never promised a specific timeline for this feature.

Can you buy $KIND token?

Yes, $KIND can be bought on Raydium DEX. However, trading comes with a warning: trading $KIND is experimental and experiential. Anyone can buy or sell the token on the open market or create pump-and-dump scenarios. Happy Kind cannot be held liable for any trading losses incurred.

We control almost all of the supply and distribute the token only to genuine shoppers and charities. The liquidity pool created by us is funded from our earnings.

Additionally, there is a 1% fee on every swap transaction. On the Raydium pool, 0.97% of it is paid to us as the liquidity provider for the USDC-KIND pool, to be added back into the liquidity pool.

How do charities benefit from $KIND token?

We believe in a future where charity and donation rely on blockchain for transparency. In real life, we do not have visibility on where our donation money goes unless the charity’s finances are audited. We foresee a future where every charity has a verified web3 wallet where donors can donate money (USDC/USDT), track how the money moves on-chain, and see when the charity withdraws to fiat.

When that day comes, Happy Kind can send $KIND tokens to genuine charities, allowing them to swap $KIND to USDC for their use. $KIND holders will also have visibility on how their 1% fee is contributed towards donations. This helps us maintain a sustainable pool to support more donations.

Don’t have a Solana wallet?

We recommend the Phantom wallet. To get started, follow the official guide: How to set up your Phantom wallet.

Don’t see $KIND in your wallet?

On Solana (SPL): add the ticker GrAwJNMMeQBrDiSVazXnPh2AYSGxdm2PQmRgrU8ojurH under Manage token list.

On Polygon (legacy bridged):

  1. Make sure your web3 wallet is connected to Polygon Mainnet.
  2. Click Import tokens.
  3. Key in 0xfeba0fa306eaa130ae60fddf41f7a7e4d2ee7630 under the Token contract address field.
  4. The Token symbol and Token decimal will be auto-populated.
  5. Click Next and you will see the $KIND token in your wallet.
