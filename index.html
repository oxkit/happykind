Who we are

Happiness and kindness, hand in hand.

The idea of Happy Kind was conceived when the founder, Joey, bought a painting for her son with a caption saying “fill the world with kindness”. In this modern world, kindness has taken a backseat to self-interest in fueling happiness.

When we started our business venture, we firmly believed that Happiness and Kindness should go hand in hand in making the world a better place.

So who are we? We run two businesses — Happy Kind Kiddo (the baby store) and KITA (the sleep studio). We sell to make a living. Just that, we make a difference by Doing Good.

Our baby store, Happy Kind Kiddo — serving Singapore and Malaysia since 2019.

What we do

Small shop, open books, kind heart.

1Share & learn

Sharing what we know and learning what we have yet to know. We engage our customers with bite-size content on our social media channels.

2Give back 1%

Kindness makes you happy and happiness makes you kind. We give back to the community by contributing 1% of sales towards our donation pool.

3Radical transparency

In open and transparency we trust. Contributions are stored in a blockchain wallet that is accessible by everyone.
