Our story

Happiness and kindness, hand in hand.

The idea of Happy Kind was conceived when the founder, Joey, bought a painting for her son with a caption saying “fill the world with kindness”.

We run Happy Kind Kiddo, our baby store serving Malaysia and Singapore since 2019. We sell to make a living and give to make a difference.
